The only production judge wire contract is `readable-semantic-2` generated by
`casepilot.compact_review.schema(contract)`. Its decoder validates against that
same schema, then `checked_semantic_review` applies evidence, unit, dependency,
version and novelty rules. Numeric scores are ordinal quality scores; semantic
states use readable labels. Binding covers the full draft and source/message spans.

`judge*.schema.json` and legacy readers in roles/review_contract/semantics remain
historical formats for archived evaluations. They are not production wire schemas.
Do not concatenate their output instructions into the active judge prompt.
Artifacts/S0 preserves the previous implementation and historical results are immutable.
